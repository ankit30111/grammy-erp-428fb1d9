Where the colours come from

176 colours are written directly into screens — text-green-600, bg-red-100 — instead of coming from the theme. That is why a "rejected" badge is not the same red on CAPA as on GRN, and why nothing responds when the brand colour changes.

What I need from you

  2. Toasts — keep use-toast and remove sonner, or the other way round?
  3. Loading — skeleton rows everywhere, or spinners?
  4. Lists — one shared table component for every list over ~20 rows, cards only for summaries. Agree?
  5. Colour — five semantic roles (accent, pass, hold, reject, muted) replacing all 176 hand-written colours. Any colour meaning I've missed?
